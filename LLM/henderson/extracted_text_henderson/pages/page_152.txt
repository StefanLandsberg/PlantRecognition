Trees & Shrubs Succulent: 1–10 m high or more

Harrisia martinii
moon cactus, harrisia cactus

Description: Spiny, succulent shrub 1–3 m high with
long, clambering, ribbed stems resembling plaited rope;
stems often arching downwards and rooting where they
touch the ground; stem sections bright green, cylindrical,
2–4 cm wide, older stems strongly tubercled (knobby
protuberances); central spine 2–3 cm long; radials 1–3(–
7), much shorter. Flowers: White, showy, 15–20 cm long,
nocturnal, Nov–Jan.

Harrisia pomanensis
midnight lady, devil's-rope cactus

Description: Very spiny, succulent shrub to 3 m high
with long, clambering stems with 4–5(–7) ribs, often
arching and even prostrate; stem sections bluish green,
cylindrical, 2–4 cm wide, not tubercled (lacking knobby
protuberances); central spine 1–2.5 cm long; radials 6–8,
± 1 cm long. Flowers: White, showy, to 15 cm long,
nocturnal. Fruits: Red, globose, non spiny, succulent
berries, 3–5 cm long. Cultivated for: Ornament.
Invades: Dry savanna, pastoral land.
