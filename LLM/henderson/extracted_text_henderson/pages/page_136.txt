Cylindropuntia pallida (C. rosea misapplied)
pink-flowered sheathed cholla

Description: Very densely spiny cactus 20–150 cm high;
stem sections cylindrical, 10–15 x 1.6–2.5 cm; spines
barbed, 1–4 cm long, covered with whitish, papery
sheaths; spines obscuring the stems. Flowers: Pink, ±
4 cm across, Nov–Jan. Fruits: Yellow, tuberculate (with
rounded protuberances), spiny, succulent berries, 2–4.5
cm long, at tips of cladodes. Cultivated for: Ornament.
Invades: Dry savanna and karoo. Origin: Mexico.

Opuntia aurantiaca
jointed cactus

Description: Spiny, much-branched, spreading,
succulent shrublet, 30–50(–200) cm high with
underground tubers; seldom exceeds 50 cm high in dry,
open veld but up to 2 m when supported in dense bush;
stem sections cylindrical to slightly flattened, bright
green, sometimes tinged a reddish purple, 6–20 x 1–3
cm, armed with sturdy, rigid, barbed spines 1–3 cm long.
Flowers: Bright yellow, outer segments red, Nov–Jan.
Fruits: Reddish, succulent berries; sterile.
