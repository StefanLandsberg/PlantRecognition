Degree of biological control after Klein (2011) and updated 2018
Complete: no other control measures are needed to reduce the weed to acceptable
levels, at least in areas where the agents have been established
Substantial: other methods are needed to reduce the weed to acceptable levels, but
less effort is required (e.g. less frequent herbicide application or less herbicide needed
per unit area)
Negligible: in spite of damage inflicted by the agents, control of the weed remains
entirely reliant on the implementation of the other control measures
Not determined: either the release of the agents has been too recent for the meaningful
evaluation, or the programme has not been evaluated
